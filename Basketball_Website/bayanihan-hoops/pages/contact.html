Frequently asked questions

Who can join the league?
Out-of-school youth and working adults in our barangay.
How much does it cost to play?
Joining is free. Sponsors cover the cost of the court and the referees.
When are games played?
Every weekend. Season 5 games are on Saturdays in November.
Can I choose my team?
No. The league committee assigns players to teams to keep the teams balanced.
Is the court accessible for players with disabilities?
Yes. The court entrance is step-free. Contact us if you need other support.
