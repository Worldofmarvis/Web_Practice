Season 5 registration is now open

Posted October 12, 2026

Registration for Season 5 is open to out-of-school youth and working adults in the barangay. Fill out the form on the Register page and the league committee will assign you to a team.

Please register before October 31 so coaches can finalize their rosters before opening day.

Rules note for scorekeepers

On the score sheet, a forfeited game is written as FF, and a game decided by fewer than three points is marked < 3 pts. Team pairs are written with an ampersand, for example Hawks & Tigers.

Opening day schedule released

Posted October 5, 2026

The Season 5 schedule is out. Opening day is Saturday, November 7, with the Barangay Hawks facing the Riverside Tigers at 3:00 PM.

The Sampaguita Stars and the Kapitbahay Knights follow at 4:30 PM. Come early for the short opening ceremony.

Covered court repainted and ready

Posted September 28, 2026

Volunteers spent two weekends repainting the lines and the key areas of our home court.

Thank you to everyone who brought brushes, snacks and good energy. The court is ready for Season 5.
